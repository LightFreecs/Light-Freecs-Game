Unity mapping (FaceShape + EyeStyle)

Field names, ranges and defaults below are the FaceShape contract. Units: 1 head unit = 1 m at character scale 1; eye line at y = 0, face points +z.

FieldMeaningMinMaxDefault

        
EyeStyle

        
Eye sizes are in head units at a cheek half-width of 0.49 and scale with the face (es = cw / 0.49). Eye centre = (±eyeSpacing, eyeY) on the face plane; brows sit browHeight above it. Saved under eyeStyle/default and eyePresets/<id>.

Derived sizes the head builder uses (port of makeShape() in this page):

        
W      = 0.5  · headWidth                (half skull width)
cw     = W    · cheekWidth               (half face width at ear/eye line)
L      = 0.565· headHeight · chinLength  (eye line → chin tip)
crownH = 0.70 · headHeight · foreheadHeight
Df,Db  = 0.46 / 0.54 · faceDepth         (front / back depth at eye line)
Front silhouette below the eye line: x = cw · profile(s), s = −y / L
  knee   k = 0.12 + 0.55·cheekFullness, width drop to knee = 0.05 + 0.17·(1 − cheekFullness)
  jaw    knee → chin with exponent 1 + 2.6·jawTaper, knee rounding radius 0.2·(1 − jawCorner)
  chin   half-width chinWidth·cw, cap height chinWidth·(0.35 + 0.9·chinPoint), round→V by chinPoint
jawCurve (optional, 25 floats): when set, it replaces the knee/jaw/chin formulas: profile(s) is a
  Catmull-Rom curve through the samples at s = 0, 1/24 … 1 (1 = cheek width at the eye line, 0 = chin tip).
profileCurve (optional, 9 floats): side view. Face-front z offset at the centre line, in cheek half-widths,
  relative to Df, at s = 0, 1/8 … 1 (Catmull-Rom through the samples). Positive = muzzle ahead of the forehead plane.
Nose: bump of 0.032·faceDepth·noseSize at y = −0.228·L: sharp step above the tip (gaussian, 0.014), straight ramp below over 0.1·L/0.6. Df = 0.46·faceDepth, Db = 0.54·(1 + 0.3·(faceDepth − 1)).
Skull above the eye line: superellipse dome, exponent n = 2 + 1.4·crownRound; back depth Db·backOfHead.
Ears at y = −0.07 + earHeight, z = −earBack, size earSize, turned forward earFlare·40°; neck radius 0.17·neckWidth, neck centre z = neckForward, shoulders start 0.08 below the chin.
