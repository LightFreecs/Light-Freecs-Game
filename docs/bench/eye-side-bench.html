What changed

  • Around each eye the forehead's forward lean is taken out, so the eye stands upright, and the eye is set 0.012 head units (about 3 mm) back.
  • Version 2: across the eye the face keeps its own smooth curve. Version 1 laid a flat plate there, and its edge made the outer top lid line wobble.
  • The nose bridge between the eyes is raised up to 0.010 head units (about 3 mm), so the eye sits behind the profile line.
  • The most it moves is 0.063 head units (about 18 mm), under the inner end of the brow. 489 of the 5,080 head points move more than 1 mm, and the cheek under the eye never comes forward.

Unchanged

  • The front view: only depth changes, and the face is painted straight from the front.
  • The painted eyes, brows, mouth, blinking and look-at, and the face shadow.
  • The hair: it is still built on the old head surface, so bangs hang the same way.
  • Every face slider and eye style. The change follows the eye's size, height and spacing, and the middle of the face never sinks.

How it was checked

The head was rebuilt in Blender from the game's own ToonHead and ToonFacePainter code, compared at 0°, 40°, 65°, 70° and 90°, and also tried on the Round + Wide, Square-Jaw + Drawn and Young + Sharp faces. The surface was also checked for creases and dents around the eye. Unity compiles with no errors; it has not been played in the game yet.

This viewer uses a simple two-tone light and outline. In the game the face is lit by its painted shadow map, so the new eye area adds no shading of its own. Mesh: ToonHead.BuildMesh on your saved face. Face: ToonFacePainter.Paint.
