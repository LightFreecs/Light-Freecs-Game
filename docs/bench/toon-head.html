Toon Head

A smooth anime head shaded the way modern cel-shaded action games do it: the face shadow comes from a painted shadow map instead of the 3D shape, eyes are painted textures, shadows are warm and tinted, and the outlines take the colour of what they outline. What makes the look

  • Painted face shadow. The face ignores its own bumps. A shadow map, painted for this face, says how far the light has to turn before each spot goes dark, so the shadow always lands in a clean shape: nose shadow first, then a curve that wraps the cheek, then half the face.
  • Smooth, simple face. Almost no nose or mouth in the mesh; the face reads from its outline, the painted features and the shadow.
  • Painted eyes. Lid line with lashes and a flick, a gradient iris (dark at the top, glowing at the bottom), pupil and two highlights, laid on the face surface.
  • Two tones with warm shadows. Lit skin and a rosy shadow tone with a soft, narrow edge, plus a faint rim light on the edges.
  • Coloured outlines. A reddish brown on skin instead of black, thinner across the face than around the head.
