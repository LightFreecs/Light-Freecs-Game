How the hair is built

  • Skull shell. A copy of the head surface pushed outward, with its own hairline per style (high at the forehead, lower at the sides, down to the nape at the back) and extra volume on top.
  • Clumps. Each spike or strand is a curved, tapered tube that ends in a point. Spikes are thick and rounded; strands are flat ribbons that lie on the head and fall over the face.
  • Toon shading. A lit tone and a dark tone with a hard edge, a tinted rim on the lit side, and a zig-zag highlight band around the crown.
  • Outline. Near-black, thinning toward the tips so points stay sharp.
