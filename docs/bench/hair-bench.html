Three anime hairstyles on the Toon Head, built the way your Light hair model is built: a handful of big, flat blades whose roots sit deep inside the skull and overlap to form the hair mass, plus a thin shell over the back of the head only. Toon shaded with a hard dark outline.

How the hair is built

  • Big blades, few of them. 16–25 per style. Each is about half the head wide at the root, flattened (wide side facing out), 8 sides, tapering straight to a point.
  • Roots inside the skull. The blades start well inside the head and overlap, so their bases form the hairline and the volume on top. No cap over the top.
  • Back shell only. A thin layer hugging the back half of the skull, from the crown to the nape.
  • Toon shading. A lit tone and a dark tone with a hard edge, a tinted rim on the lit side, and a zig-zag highlight band around the crown.
  • Outline. Near-black, thinning toward the tips so points stay sharp.
