Attack Bench

The basic 4-hit combo, redone after your clip: a rush of straight punches that shoot out in a couple of frames and hold, the other fist tight at the chest, then a thrusting kick to finish. Moving limbs get ink speed lines over a pale stretched copy of the limb, hits get yellow sparks, and the finisher gets a speed-line background. The body stays solid: no spins, small turns. This is the game's own body and skeleton, posed by the same rig maths the game uses.

Tap the stage, press J or the button. Same timing as the game: next hit after 0.25 s, combo resets after 0.6 s.
