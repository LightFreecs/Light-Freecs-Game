The basic 4-hit combo, rebuilt in a shonen fight style: a quick wind-up, a snap to the hit with almost no in-between frames, a frozen impact frame, follow-through, then back to guard. This is the game's own body and 42-bone skeleton, posed by the same rig maths the game uses, so what you see here is what goes in.

Tap the stage, press J or the button. Same timing as the game: next hit after 0.25 s, combo resets after 0.6 s.
