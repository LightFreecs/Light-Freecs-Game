Locomotion Bench

The run from your clip and a walk to go with it, on the game's own body and skeleton. The run copies the clip: the body leans about 50° forward and stays low, the back leg kicks up level with the hips, one fist is tucked under the chin while the other arm is thrown straight back with the hand open, at the clip's six steps a second. Walking is the default; hold Shift to run, like in the game. Locked on, it sidesteps, backpedals and runs backwards while facing the opponent. The floor moves at the fighter's real speed, so a planted foot should stay on its tile.

Hold Shift while walking to run, let go to walk again. J or Punch throws the basic combo, standing or on the move (the legs keep going).

Locked on

The fighter faces the opponent (the faint figure ahead) and moves the way you pick, like holding W A S D while locked on with Tab. Walking sidesteps and backpedals; running turns the legs into the run with the eyes kept on the opponent, and backs away with a backwards run. Best in the Game camera and ¾ views.

Numbers

The clip's run is drawn on ones: four drawings per step, a step every 0.17 s. In the drawings a foot stays down for most of the step, which only works because the background slides; at the game's 8 m/s the foot would skate. So the run keeps the clip's poses and rhythm but the foot is down for 45 % of the step and the rest is a short leap, which makes the planted foot travel exactly as fast as the ground.

Shoulders

The skin on top of the shoulder used to follow the upper arm almost completely, and the collarbone never moved, so any raised arm (a punch, the guard) dragged the top of the shoulder backwards and the arm looked like it came out of the chest. Now the shoulder top and trapezius belong to the collarbone, and the collarbone lifts and rolls forward a little with the arm. Switch New shoulders off to compare, best in the Shoulders view while punching.

Already in the game

  • Walking is the default; hold Shift to run. On a gamepad, click the left stick and it runs until you let go of the stick; on touch, push the stick to its edge or hold RUN. Walk 2.2 m/s, run 8 m/s, as on this page.
  • Every fighter uses the Shonen Sprint style. Styles live in one list, so more can be added later and picked per fighter, like the basic-attack movesets.
  • Walking and running blend with the speed; in a jump or a dash the stride holds.
  • Locked on (Tab) the fighter keeps facing the opponent. Walking sidesteps (a low shuffle, the feet never cross) and backpedals; a diagonal blends its two neighbours, each turned to the exact direction, so a planted foot never skates. Running turns the legs and hips into the run while the chest and eyes stay on the opponent; past 120° it backs away with a backwards run instead, with a quick hip flip between the two.
  • Running sets the gritted-teeth Determined face from the clip; it goes back to neutral when you stop.
  • The new shoulders in every pose: idle, guard, punches, the run.
