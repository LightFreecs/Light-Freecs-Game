Low-Poly Head

Fighters are built from swappable parts: a head shape, a nose and a pair of ears, each its own piece that snaps onto fixed points on the head. Eyes and hair come later as more parts. Mostly quads, so every piece opens cleanly in Blockbench and still looks smooth in the toon shader.

    How the parts fit

    • Each head shape carries two attach points: the nose point (where the nose tip sits) and the ear point (one per side). Noses and ears are built around those points, so any nose or ear fits any head.
    • Noses and ears hug the head surface underneath them, so they sit flush whatever the cheek or skull curve.
    • The neck is a short stub cut at the bottom, where the body's neck will join.

    Open it in Blockbench

    Copy the OBJ for the combination shown, save it as head.obj, then in Blockbench use File › Import › OBJ Model. It comes in as four parts: head, nose, ear_L, ear_R. The default (Youth, Pointed nose, Round ears) is also in the project at docs/bench/low-poly-head.obj.
