Charge Aura

The flame aura shown while charging (hold Charge) and, bigger, during Power Surge. The outline is traced from the reference aura: a flame pine tree of seven tiers a side, wide wings low down and blades that stand up straighter the higher they sit, with a narrow spire and one peak over the head. The fighter stands in a see-through arch. Every tier tip licks out and snaps back, the outline boils like hand-drawn frames, the peak flickers, and small flicks rise inside. It is drawn flat, facing the camera: the flame behind the fighter and bright dashes flying up in front. The flame moves 24 times a second and the outline boils 12 times a second.
