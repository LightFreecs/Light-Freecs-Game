Charge Aura

The flame aura shown while charging (hold Charge) and, bigger, during Power Surge. The outline is traced from the reference aura: a flame pine tree of seven tiers a side, wide wings low down and blades that stand up straighter the higher they sit, with a narrow spire and one peak over the head. The fighter stands in a see-through arch. The tiers rise like the reference clip: the bottom wings climb to become the middle tiers and the middle ones the top, the top one melts into the spire and a new one grows out of the base. The outline is redrawn in hand-drawn steps, about 9 drawings a second (4 per rise, 0.45 s, as in the clip), so it also boils; the peak flickers and small flicks rise inside. It is drawn flat, facing the camera: the flame behind the fighter and bright dashes flying up in front. Streaks and flicks move 24 times a second. Pick a colour to preview the Customize choices.
