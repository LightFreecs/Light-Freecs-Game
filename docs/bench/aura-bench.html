Charge Aura

The flame aura shown while charging (hold Charge) and, bigger, during Power Surge. The flame hugs the body like a tall teardrop and ends in one sharp peak well over the head. Flame spikes race up both sides and melt into the peak, the peak sways and flickers, and streaks rise inside. It is drawn flat, facing the camera: the flame behind the fighter and bright dashes flying up in front. The drawing changes 24 times a second, like hand-drawn anime frames.
